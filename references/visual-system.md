# Visual System

## Reference reading

The bundled reference demonstrates the portrait-source layout in a quiet 4:3 poster:

- left 50%: a uniform dusty blue information field;
- right 50%: a full-height photographic crop;
- left motif: a small antique-gold enamel souvenir pin showing the animal before a mountain contour;
- text: one centered uppercase monospaced line below the badge;
- hierarchy: photograph first, badge second, microtype third;
- large left-side negative space makes the badge feel collected rather than advertised.

## Transferable rules

- Adapt structure to source orientation while preserving the restrained hierarchy:
  - landscape source -> portrait 3:4 canvas, photograph above, information field below;
  - portrait or square source -> landscape 4:3 canvas, information field left, photograph right.
- Use a flush 50/50 split in both structures.
- Prefer a near-matching canvas that lets the photograph span its field with no crop or only a subtle crop. Fall back to a complete contain fit for extreme aspect ratios.
- Keep the primary subject fully visible with comfortable breathing room; subject preservation overrides edge-to-edge fill.
- Derive the left color from a broad photo field such as sky, water, stone, foliage, fog, or wall.
- Keep the information panel uniform and slightly subdued so the badge remains legible.
- Reduce the photo to one badge subject plus one setting cue. Examples: bird + reeds, person + ridge, boat + wave, tower + tree, cat + fence.
- Use antique-gold as structure, not as a large fill. Limit enamel colors to those supported by the current photo.
- Place the badge near the optical center of the information panel, slightly above the text.
- Keep the text factual and typographically quiet.

## Text formatting

- Line format: `PLACE | SUBJECT | MON DDTH YYYY`.
- Uppercase only.
- Use a monospaced or typewriter-like face with generous tracking.
- Keep the line centered and comfortably within the information field.
- Use white or a very light warm neutral when contrast permits; otherwise use a dark sampled neutral.
- Ordinal suffixes: `1ST`, `2ND`, `3RD`, `4TH` … `21ST`, `22ND`, `23RD`, `24TH`.

## Avoid

- photo collage, torn paper, postcard borders, film frames, maps, stamps, tape, labels, swatches;
- gradients, glow, large shadows, glossy 3D mockups, thick outlines unrelated to enamel metal;
- multiple text blocks or oversized display typography;
- literal copying of the bundled moose and mountain badge.
